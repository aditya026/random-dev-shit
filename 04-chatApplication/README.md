# 🧠 Active Recall Learning App

Welcome to the **My Active Recall Learning App**! This application is designed to help users learn and remember topics more effectively through the proven scientific method of *active recall*. Instead of passively reading or highlighting, users engage with their material dynamically to build true mastery.

## 🚀 How It Works

Our app takes your study materials and turns them into an interactive learning experience. Users can provide:
- 📖 **A specific topic** 
- 📄 **A book or document** (PDF uploads)
- 📝 **Personal study notes** (Text input)
- 🔗 **Any other learning material** you want to master

The application's AI analyzes the provided content and automatically generates highly targeted questions to test your knowledge.

## 🎮 Two Learning Modes

Tailor your study session to your current knowledge level:

### 🟢 Warm-Up Mode
*Perfect for beginners and first-time learners.*
- The app asks straightforward, foundational questions.
- Designed to build basic understanding and reinforce key, high-level concepts without overwhelming the user.

### 🔴 Boss-Battle Mode
*Designed for deeper learning and true mastery.*
- Challenges users with advanced, thought-provoking questions.
- Tests true comprehension, application of knowledge, critical thinking, and long-term retention.

## ⚡ Instant, Formative Feedback

Learning doesn't stop when you get a question wrong. The app acts as an intuitive personal tutor:
- **✅ Correct Answer:** Great job! You seamlessly move on to the next question.
- **❌ Incorrect or Stuck:** The app immediately provides a simple, easy-to-understand explanation of the concept before continuing. This ensures users actually *learn* from their mistakes rather than just rote memorizing correct answers.

## 🎯 Our Goal

The main objective of this application is to make studying **interactive** and **enjoyable**. By leveraging question-based learning (active recall), we aim to help users retain information significantly better and longer than traditional passive reading methods.
