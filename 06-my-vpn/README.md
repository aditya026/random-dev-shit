# Create my own VPN

## what is VPN
A VPN(virtual private network) is a service that encrypt your Internet Connection and routes it through a remote server, hiding your IP address and secure your online Identity.

#### The VPN Process
* **Encryption:** Your device scrambles data before sending.
* **Tunneling:** Encrypted data travels through a secure path.
* **IP Masking:** Server hides your location with a new IP.
* **Decryption:** Server unscrambles and forwards data to the website.
* **Response Reversal:** Website response is encrypted again on return.

#### Types
* **Remote Access VPN:-** You're at a café and want to access your college/company network.
* **Site-to-Site VPN:-** The individual computers don't necessarily need VPN software.
* **Client-to-Site VPN:-** The employee uses a VPN client to enter the organization's network.
* **SSL VPN:-** Some SSL VPNs provide full network-level access through a client, while others provide access only to specific applications.
